# 00 — Product Vision & Brand Story

**Decision:** Product brand is **Light Luna**, alongside Light Remote and LightBI in the Light Family. Keep the spelling as two words; do not rename it LightLuna, FrogWall or Light Sentinel. Luna is the primary intelligent defense persona, Selene may become a distinct independent evaluation/research agent. The brand is not exclusively dependent on a particular AI model.

## Why the Moon?

- **Earth = protected infrastructure:** sites, hosts, networks and services need continuous availability.
- **Moon's orbit = patrol:** recurrent observation across nights, days and service lifecycles, including local and cloud edges.
- **Moon = interconnected system:** tidal influence illustrates how a companion system can impact a larger ecosystem; security controls have consequences across services.
- **Meteor showers and impacts = botnet noise, scans and incidents:** threats continuously arrive; a defensive system measures and responds rather than treating all events as catastrophic.
- **Craters = evidence and memory:** verified attacks, containment results and false positives create learning opportunities, with retention and privacy limits.
- **Phases, eclipses, blood moon = vivid operator state:** gradients from calm to threat, plus a distinct telemetry-blind state.
- **Cultural resonance:** phases, Mid-Autumn Festival, moonlight and eclipse stories offer long-term design storytelling, not literal security claims.

**Scientific accuracy note:** the Moon is not a shield that intercepts most objects bound for Earth. Impact history is a *metaphor*, not an astrophysical explanation of Earth's safety. The Moon contributes to tides, but Earth's climate and habitability have many drivers.

## Positioning

> **Light Luna — Orbit. Observe. Defend.**
>
> A reliable traditional software firewall, enhanced by explainable, evidence-led autonomous security.

Target adopters: self-hosters, SMEs, VPS operators, Linux administrators, private clusters, edge-site operators and later OEM/mini-PC firewall installers. Do not conflate Light Luna with NetBird, CrowdSec or OPNsense: compete on coherent workflows and verified outcomes, not by claiming to replace all vendor engines in v1.

## Product vs Brain vs Cloud

- **Light Luna:** installable firewall and UI. Must remain safe with AI off and Cloud disconnected.
- **Luna Brain:** bounded analyses, baseline learning, hypotheses, evidence grading, recommendation and feedback; can run local light models/rules; no direct privileged shell.
- **Light Luna Cloud:** optional signed intelligence, fleet control, update catalog and privacy-conscious opt-in shared signals. Subscription model TBD.
- **Selene:** optional shadow reviewer/red-team *reasoning critic* under defensive scope and policy gate; not an uncontrolled self-modifying agent.

## Success is measured by

Deterministic policy semantics; rule change safety; zero unexpected admin lockouts in test matrix; ability to restore connectivity; measured false positive/false negative results on independently labeled data; sustainable memory and bandwidth; comprehensible operator decisions; secure installation and updates.

## Not now

No dedicated hardware engineering, no always-on cloud dependency, no auto patching of production hosts, no covert traffic interception, no required two-NIC setup for Edge Mode, no promise of general-purpose protection from every CVE, and no shipping AI-enabled real bans before controlled tests and explicit activation.
